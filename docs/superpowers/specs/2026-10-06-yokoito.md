that will replace rupu's workflow, autoflow and agentiflow definitions — lives
in its own repository:

- **Spec:** [`docs/spec/00-index.md`](https://github.com/Section9Labs/yokoito/blob/main/docs/spec/00-index.md)

- **Legacy parity:** [`docs/spec/C-legacy-parity.md`](https://github.com/Section9Labs/yokoito/blob/main/docs/spec/C-legacy-parity.md) — every current rupu workflow/autoflow/agentiflow feature → its yokoito form
